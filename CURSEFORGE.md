**Your leveling route one step at a time: what to do now, where to go, and what to keep doing on the way.**

Itinerary is a leveling companion for WoW Forever: a route panel with the current step and live objective counts, a waypoint arrow, and automation for the busywork in between. **It ships without routes:** record your own, import a shared one, or install a route pack (a separate add-on).

## Getting started

Unzip into `Interface\AddOns` (two folders: **Itinerary** and **Itinerary_Data**) and log in. No route yet? `/iti recorder` and press Record, or `/iti import` a shared one. Minimap compass: click shows the panel, Shift + click the settings.

## Panel, arrow and buttons

- The **current step** with hints and live counts, clickable trainer and vendor errands, **along the way** objectives, the next steps and your **pace**
- A **waypoint arrow** and your **next stops as pins** on the map and minimap
- **Item buttons** for quest items and the hearthstone; **target buttons** per kind of mob, and a key that targets the nearest and tags it with a spell of your choice
- **Name tags** on quest objects you can click

## Automation and helpers

Every part can be switched off; hold **Shift** at an NPC to do it by hand.

- **Quests**: accept, hand in, click through dialogue, mark the best-selling reward
- **Vendors and bank**: sell grey items; the route's bank steps
- **Travel**: flights, hearthstone, spirit healer on death skips
- **Trainers**: a note when one has something for you or you have talent points; can buy class skills, keeping money back
- **Professions**: learn them, buy the gathering tools and your first profession's apprentice bag, put straight into your reagent bag slot
- **Groups**: share the quests you accept; escorts wait for the group
- **Gather mode** (`/iti gather`): a farming route between herb or ore spots

## Make your own routes

- **Recorder** (`/iti recorder`): writes a route while you play
- **Editor** (`/iti edit`): every step type, conditions, hints, waypoints, kill areas, dialogue and macro buttons
- **Share** as text (`/iti export`, `/iti import`) or write it as JSON (`/iti dump`)

More: `/iti` panel, `/iti settings`, `/iti next` / `/iti back`, `/iti routes`, `/iti debug`.
