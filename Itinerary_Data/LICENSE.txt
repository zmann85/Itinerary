Itinerary - Data
================

Where trainers, weapon masters and tool vendors stand, where herbs and ore veins can spawn, and which
objects give the items a quest asks for, for Itinerary (trainer instructions, gathering tools, gather mode,
object name tags).

The positions come from the WoW Forever data of QuestieDB (Questie, https://github.com/Questie/Questie,
GNU General Public License v3) and from nodes and quest objects Itinerary players found in game. This data add-on is
distributed under the GNU GPL v3 accordingly (full text in COPYING.txt). It comes in the same download as
Itinerary, in a folder of its own, but is separate from Itinerary itself (all rights reserved); Itinerary
works without it and records what you meet in game.
